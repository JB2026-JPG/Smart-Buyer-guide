HOW IT WORKS

Independent research behind every guide.

We look at the kinds of questions people are already asking in public about business tools, and use that to prioritize which guides to write and keep updated.

We start from real questions

Guides are prioritized based on topics people are genuinely asking about, not what pays the most.

We never contact anyone

No individuals are ever messaged, profiled, or identified as part of this research.

Advice comes first

Each guide is written around real buying decisions—cost, fit and exit terms—regardless of whether an affiliate link is present.

Transparency: Some guides may include a clearly disclosed affiliate link. This never changes the recommendations above it.
