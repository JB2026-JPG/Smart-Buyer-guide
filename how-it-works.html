How we evaluate real costs

Software pricing pages often hide the true long-term price behind aggressive intro discounts and complex tier calculations.

Our evaluation process

  • Renewal Rate Tracking: We look beyond the first year discount to calculate total 3-year ownership costs.
  • Feature Lockouts: We identify critical features (like SSL, backups, or DKIM setup) that require paid add-ons.
  • Tier Escalation: We map out what happens to your bill when your contacts, traffic, or team size increases.
