THE METHOD

Research signals. Useful guidance. No cold contact.

The system is designed to identify public questions that may indicate a commercial problem, then route them to useful buyer information.

01 · SOURCE

Public questions

We query the public Stack Exchange Software Recommendations API. Only information already made public by the source is used.

02 · SCORE

Buyer intent

Words such as buy, price, compare, alternative and urgency raise the score. Purely informational language lowers it.

03 · ROUTE

Topic matching

Questions are routed to CRM, hosting or broader business-software guidance based on their wording.

04 · CTA

Affiliate option

If you have an approved affiliate URL configured, the relevant opportunity can display a clearly disclosed sponsored link.

What the machine does not do: it does not scrape profiles, collect personal contact details, send messages, pretend a person is a confirmed buyer, or invent affiliate links.
Source data is research material, not proof of purchase intent. Verify current provider terms before purchasing. · Affiliate disclosure · Privacy
