The new metric to add: Cost Shock

Cost Shock is an analytical metric, not a provider rating. It measures the percentage increase in effective recurring cost between two defined scenarios.

Cost Shock % = (Growth scenario annual cost − Entry scenario annual cost) ÷ Entry scenario annual cost × 100

Always publish the assumptions next to the percentage. A high Cost Shock is not automatically bad; it may reflect a buyer deliberately adding valuable capabilities.

The second metric: Commitment Cost

Commitment Cost is the estimated total subscription and material known extras over a defined period. It should never pretend to include unknown migration or implementation costs unless they are explicitly estimated.

Commitment Cost = subscription + known recurring extras + known one-time fees
