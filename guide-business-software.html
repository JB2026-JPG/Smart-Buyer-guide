How SaaSRealCost compares business software

The Real Cost Score™ is designed to answer a simple question: what will this software really cost you as your business grows?

1. Starting Price How affordable is the entry point?
2. Growth Cost What happens to the price as users, contacts or usage increase?
3. Hidden Extras Which important features or services cost more?
4. Cost Transparency How easy is it to understand the actual pricing?
5. Ease of Exit How easy is it to leave without unnecessary cost or lock-in?

Each criterion receives equal weighting: 20%. The final score is therefore based on the same methodology for every provider.

What to check before buying business software

  • Don't compare only the advertised starting price. Calculate the price at the size you expect to reach.
  • Check what happens when you grow. Seat-based, contact-based and usage-based pricing can change the economics quickly.
  • Look for mandatory extras. Onboarding, additional contacts, automation, integrations and usage credits can all affect total cost.
  • Check the renewal structure. A low introductory price is not necessarily a low long-term price.
  • Consider the exit route. Your software choice should remain financially manageable if you later decide to switch.

Why the Real Cost Score™ matters

Two software products can offer similar features while having very different long-term costs. The Real Cost Score™ is designed to make those differences easier to see before you commit.
