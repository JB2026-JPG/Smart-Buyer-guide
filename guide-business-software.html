Frequently asked

How many tools does a small business actually need?

Start with the smallest set that covers your core workflow. Adding tools later is easier than untangling overlapping subscriptions.

Should I choose an all-in-one suite or separate tools?

All-in-one suites reduce integration work but can be weaker in any single area. Separate best-in-class tools cost more to connect but often perform better individually.

What's the risk of annual billing?

Lower per-month cost, but less flexibility if the tool turns out not to fit. Consider a monthly trial period first.
