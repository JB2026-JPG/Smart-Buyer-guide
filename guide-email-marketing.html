EMAIL MARKETING GUIDE

Choose email marketing software that fits your list size.

Most email tools price by subscriber count, so the right choice today can get expensive fast as your list grows.

Affiliate disclosure: This page may contain a sponsored affiliate link. It is only activated when an approved tracking URL is configured.

1. Check the pricing curve

Look at pricing not just for your current list size, but for 2-3x that size. Some tools stay cheap early and become expensive fast at scale.

2. Check deliverability reputation

A cheap tool with poor deliverability means your emails land in spam. Look for providers with strong sender reputation and authentication support (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).

3. Check automation depth

  • Welcome sequences and drip campaigns
  • Behavior-based triggers
  • List segmentation options

4. Check your exit route

Verify you can export your full subscriber list and campaign history if you switch providers later.

Frequently asked

Why did my email bill suddenly increase?

Most providers bill by subscriber count. Crossing a pricing tier threshold—even by one contact—can bump you to the next price bracket.

Should I clean my list before switching?

Yes—removing inactive subscribers before migrating reduces cost and improves deliverability on the new platform.
