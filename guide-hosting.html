Frequently asked

Why did my hosting bill increase after year one?

Most hosts advertise a discounted first-term price. Renewal pricing is often listed separately—check it before signing up.

Do I need managed hosting?

If you don't want to handle server maintenance yourself, managed hosting is worth the premium. For simple static sites, shared hosting is usually enough.

What should I check before migrating providers?

Confirm the new host supports a full backup import, and keep the old host active until DNS has fully propagated.
