How the Real Cost Score™ works

Every factor has equal weight.

Price 20% + Growth cost 20% + Hidden extras 20% + Cost transparency 20% + Ease of exit 20%

The five scores are averaged to create the final score out of 10.

Example: 9 + 8 + 8 + 9 + 9 = 43 ÷ 5 = 8.6/10.

The five criteria

Price

20%

How attractive is the actual entry cost for the product being compared?

Growth cost

20%

What happens to the cost when users, features or business requirements grow?

Hidden extras

20%

Can add-ons, credits, onboarding, storage or other extras materially change the final bill?

Transparency

20%

How clearly does the provider explain plans, limits, billing and extras?

Ease of exit

20%

How difficult is it to cancel, export data and move to another provider?

Our research rule

We do not give a provider a high score simply because its first advertised price is low.

The purpose of the Real Cost Score™ is to answer a different question: what is this product likely to cost and feel like as your needs develop?

Scores are reviewed when material pricing, plan or product changes occur.

Pricing checked today — September 15, 2026. Prices, plans, limits, discounts, taxes, add-ons and fees can change at any time and may already be different the next day. Always verify current provider pricing before committing.
