How the Real Cost Score™ works

Every provider is evaluated using the same five factors. The goal is not simply to reward the cheapest CRM.

Price What does the starting plan actually cost?
Growth cost What happens when users, contacts or usage grow?
Hidden extras What additional products, fees or upgrades matter?
Cost transparency How easy is it to understand the actual bill?
Ease of exit How difficult or expensive is it to leave later?

Why we don't rank providers on price alone

The cheapest CRM is not automatically the cheapest CRM for your business.

A low entry price can become less attractive when you need more users, more contacts, automation, integrations or additional functionality.

That is why the Real Cost Score™ is designed to look at the full buying decision rather than just the number displayed on a pricing page.
