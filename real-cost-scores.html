The Five Real Cost Criteria

Every scored provider is evaluated against the same methodology. Each criterion represents 20% of the final score.

1. Starting Price How attractive and accessible is the entry-level price?
2. Growth Cost What happens to the cost as users, contacts or usage increase?
3. Hidden Extras Are important features, add-ons or services charged separately?
4. Cost Transparency How clearly does the provider explain what customers actually pay?
5. Ease of Exit How easy is it to downgrade, cancel or move away?
