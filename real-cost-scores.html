How the Real Cost Score™ Works

The Real Cost Score is designed to look beyond the advertised starting price. Every provider is evaluated using the same five criteria, with each criterion receiving equal weight.

Starting Cost 20%

How attractive and realistic the entry price is.

Growth Cost 20%

What happens to the cost as users, customers or needs increase.

Hidden Extras 20%

Important additional costs, limitations or paid extras.

Transparency 20%

How clearly pricing, renewals and important conditions are communicated.

Ease of Exit 20%

How easy it is to cancel, leave or move to another provider.

Independent by Methodology

SaaSRealCost may use affiliate links, but affiliate relationships do not determine the score. Every provider is evaluated using the same five criteria. Every provider we research is eligible to be published, regardless of the outcome.
