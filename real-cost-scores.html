How the Real Cost Score works

The Real Cost Score is designed to look beyond the advertised starting price. Every provider is evaluated using the same five criteria, with each criterion receiving equal weight.

Starting Cost How attractive and realistic the entry price is.
Growth Cost What happens to the cost as usage, customers or needs increase.
Hidden Extras Important additional costs, limitations or paid extras.
Transparency How clearly pricing, renewals and important conditions are communicated.
Ease of Exit How easy it is to cancel, leave or move to another provider.

Overall Score = (Starting Cost + Growth Cost + Hidden Extras + Transparency + Ease of Exit) ÷ 5

Independent by methodology

SaaS Real Cost may use affiliate links, but affiliate relationships do not determine the score. Every provider is evaluated using the same five criteria. Every provider we research is eligible to be published, regardless of the outcome.
