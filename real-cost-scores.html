THE FIVE FACTORS

One framework. Five equally weighted factors.

Every provider in a category is evaluated using the same Real Cost Score™ framework.

Price — 20% How attractive is the actual entry cost?
Growth cost — 20% What happens as users, features or requirements grow?
Hidden extras — 20% Can add-ons, credits, onboarding or storage materially change the bill?
Cost transparency — 20% How clearly are plans, limits, billing and extras explained?
Ease of exit — 20% How difficult is cancellation, export and migration?
