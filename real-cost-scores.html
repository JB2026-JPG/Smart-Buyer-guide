How We Calculate the Score

Every provider is assessed using the same five questions. No single low introductory price can hide a difficult long-term cost.

1

Starting Cost

How attractive is the real starting price, considering the plan, billing term and conditions?

2

Growth Cost

What happens to the bill as your usage, subscribers, contacts or website grows?

3

Hidden Extras

Are important features, add-ons, limits or overages likely to increase the bill?

4

Transparency

Can a buyer understand the pricing and important conditions before committing?

5

Ease of Exit

How easy is it to cancel, downgrade or move away when the provider no longer fits?

What Makes This Different?

We are not asking: "Which tool has the most features?"

We are asking a buyer question: "What is this choice likely to cost me once I actually start using it?"

That means a tool can have a low advertised price and still receive a lower Real Cost Score if growth, limits, add-ons or pricing complexity make the long-term cost harder to predict.

A Real Cost Score is not a product review

The Real Cost Score measures the financial reality of a provider under our five criteria. It does not attempt to determine which provider has the best features, design, performance or overall customer experience.

A higher score means the provider is more favorable under the SaaS Real Cost methodology based on these five financial criteria.
