SaaSRealCost exists to answer one question honestly: what will this software actually cost you — not just today, but as you grow? Here's exactly how we research, score, and stay accountable.

Why SaaSRealCost exists

Advertised pricing rarely tells the full story. A low starting price can hide steep costs once you add seats, contacts, features, or usage. Providers aren't required to make that easy to compare — so we do it for you.

SaaSRealCost was built to look past the headline price and evaluate what a piece of software really costs over time: as your team grows, as your usage increases, and if you ever need to leave.

The Real Cost Score™ framework

Every provider we cover is evaluated against the same five criteria, each weighted equally. No provider is scored on a different scale, and no criterion is adjusted to favor a particular outcome.

How we research each provider

  1. Pull current pricing directly from the source We start with the provider's own official pricing page — not third-party aggregators — to record current plans, seat costs, and billing terms.
  2. Identify add-ons and growth triggers We map out what happens as usage grows: additional seats, contact tiers, feature gates, and optional add-ons that aren't included in the base price.
  3. Score against the five-criteria framework Each criterion is scored independently on a 10-point scale, then combined into the final Real Cost Score using equal 20% weighting.
  4. Write the verdict We summarize who the provider is genuinely a good fit for, and what to watch out for before committing.
  5. Recheck on a regular cycle Pricing changes. We revisit provider pages periodically and note the date pricing was last verified on every page.

Editorial independence

Our scores are based on publicly available pricing and terms, assessed against the same framework for every provider — regardless of whether we have a commercial relationship with them. A provider cannot pay for a higher score, and our research is published whether the outcome is favorable to them or not.

How we make money

SaaSRealCost is free to use and supported primarily through affiliate commissions. When you click through to a provider and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

This never influences our scoring. Affiliate relationships are evaluated using the exact same five-criteria framework as every other provider we cover, and commission rates play no role in how a provider is scored.

What we don't do

✕ Accept payment for a higher score
✕ Hide or omit unfavorable findings
✕ Copy scores from other review sites
✕ Use outdated pricing without verifying it

Corrections & updates

Pricing changes constantly, and providers sometimes restructure plans without notice. If you spot pricing on our site that no longer matches what a provider currently charges, we want to know — accuracy is the entire point of SaaSRealCost.
