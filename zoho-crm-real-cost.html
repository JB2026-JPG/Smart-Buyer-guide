The SaaSRealCost verdict

Zoho scores highly because it combines a broad CRM feature set with a multi-tier pricing structure and a detailed pricing calculator.

Its biggest Real Cost lesson is that the advertised user price is only one component. Storage, support, portals, backup and other requirements can change the final amount.

Best suited for: businesses looking for broad CRM functionality and strong value.

Watch carefully: add-ons, storage, support and local taxes.
