Current Zoho CRM pricing

USD pricing checked September 15, 2026.

Free: $0 for up to 3 users.

Standard: $14 per user/month, billed annually.

Professional: $23 per user/month, billed annually.

Enterprise: $40 per user/month, billed annually.

Ultimate: $52 per user/month, billed annually.

Zoho also offers monthly billing at higher per-user rates. The final price can depend on the billing period, region and selected edition.

Additional cost can come from team users, storage, support, backup, portals and other requirements. Zoho currently lists Team User licenses at $9 per user/month when billed annually or $11 per user/month when billed monthly.

Local taxes such as VAT or GST are charged in addition to the listed prices.

The SaaSRealCost verdict

Zoho scores highly because it combines a broad CRM feature set with a multi-tier pricing structure and a detailed pricing system.

Its biggest Real Cost lesson is that the advertised user price is only one component. Storage, support, portals, team users and other requirements can change the final amount.

Best suited for: businesses looking for broad CRM functionality and strong value.

Watch carefully: add-ons, storage, support, team users and local taxes.
