What to check before you buy

Where can the real cost increase?

1. Renewal

The introductory price is temporary. Renewal pricing is therefore one of the most important numbers to check before committing to a long-term hosting provider.

2. Add-ons

A hosting plan can include many useful features, but you may also encounter optional products or upgrades. Only add services you actually need.

3. Website growth

A small personal website and a growing business website do not necessarily have the same requirements. Storage, performance, security, traffic and support needs can change as your site grows.

4. Your time

The cheapest hosting plan isn't always the cheapest solution if it creates technical problems, migration work or additional services later.
