What changes after the promotion?

The most important real-cost issue is the gap between the introductory rate and the renewal rate. For example, Premium moves from an effective $2.99/month during the 48-month promotional term to $10.99/month at renewal. That is more than three times the introductory monthly figure.

This does not make Hostinger unusually expensive for hosting, but it does mean that comparing only the first-year or headline monthly price can give the wrong impression of long-term cost.

What is included?

  • SSL is included with the hosting plans.
  • Premium includes up to 3 websites and 20 GB SSD storage.
  • Business includes up to 50 websites, 50 GB NVMe storage and daily backups.
  • Eligible plans include a domain free for the first year.
  • Hostinger provides a 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • Some email and AI features are included only for a limited period or depend on the plan.

Who should consider Hostinger?

Hostinger is particularly attractive for people who want a low initial hosting cost and are comfortable paying for a longer term upfront. It becomes less attractive if you want the lowest possible commitment, expect to move hosts quickly, or compare providers using only the promotional monthly number.

Before you commit

  • Calculate the full amount due today, not just the monthly headline price.
  • Write down the renewal price before purchasing.
  • Check how many websites, storage and backups you actually need.
  • Check whether included email, domain and AI features remain free for the whole term.
  • Compare the renewal cost with alternatives before deciding that the promotional rate is the best long-term deal.
