Three questions before you buy

1. What will I pay later? Check renewal prices, billing periods and paid add-ons.
2. Does it fit my actual job? Ignore features you will never use. Buy for the workflow you have.
3. Can I leave easily? Check cancellation, refunds, data export and migration before committing.
