Use the Real Cost Check before you buy.

Instead of asking only “How much does it cost?” ask three questions that reveal whether the product actually makes sense for you.

1

What will it really cost?

Look beyond the introductory price. Check renewals, required extras, users, storage, transactions and other costs that can appear as your needs change.

2

Does it fit the job?

A cheap tool can become expensive if it lacks something important. Compare the features you actually need instead of paying for everything on the list.

3

Can you leave easily?

Switching costs matter. Check contracts, data portability, migration effort and how difficult it may be to move somewhere else later.

What people often discover too late.

The advertised price is only one part of the buying decision.

Renewals

The price that attracts you today may not be the price you pay later.

Add-ons

Important functionality can sometimes require another plan or paid feature.

Growth

More customers, contacts, storage or usage can change your monthly bill.

Switching

Leaving a platform can involve migration work, lost data or rebuilding workflows.
