What the Real Cost Score measures

A low starting price does not automatically mean low real cost.

1. Starting Price How attractive and accessible is the entry-level price?
2. Growth Cost What happens to the bill as users, contacts or usage increase?
3. Hidden Extras Which important features or capabilities may cost extra?
4. Transparency How clearly does the provider explain pricing, limits and conditions?
5. Ease of Exit How easy is it to downgrade, cancel or move away?

Our approach

SaaSRealCost is designed to make software pricing easier to understand before you commit.

We look beyond the headline price

A provider can advertise an attractive starting price while the cost changes significantly when you add users, contacts, features or other requirements.

Our research focuses on those changes so you can make a better-informed decision.

One scoring framework

  • Starting price
  • Growth cost
  • Hidden extras
  • Cost transparency
  • Ease of exit

Each criterion contributes 20% to the final Real Cost Score™.
