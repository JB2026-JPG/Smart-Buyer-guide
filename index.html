REAL COST CHECK

Three questions to ask before you buy.

1. What will it really cost?

Check renewals, users, storage, add-ons, credits, taxes and other costs that can appear as your needs change.

2. Does it fit the job?

A cheap tool can become expensive if it lacks something important. Compare what you actually need.

3. Can you leave easily?

Check cancellation, data export, migration effort and contract terms before committing.
