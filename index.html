2

Register your domain

During sign-up you'll be asked to search for a domain name. If you don't have one yet, this is where you claim it — it's included free for the first year on most plans. Already own a domain elsewhere? You can enter it instead and point it to Bluehost afterward.

  • Keep it short and easy to say out loud
  • .com is still the safest default for a public-facing blog
  • Avoid hyphens and numbers if you can — they're harder to share verbally
3

Create your account and check out

You'll fill in account details, pick your billing term, and land on an extras page — backups, domain privacy, SiteLock security. None of these are required to launch; skip them at first and add anything you actually need later once you know your site's traffic and needs.

A longer billing term lowers the monthly rate but locks in more upfront. If you're not sure the blog will stick, a 12-month term is a safer test than 36.
4

Install WordPress

Bluehost sets WordPress up automatically as part of onboarding — you'll land straight in the WordPress dashboard after checkout. If you ever need to install it manually or add a second site, it's a one-click option from the hosting control panel.

5

Pick a theme and publish your first post

Start with a free, simple theme — you can change it later without losing content. Write and publish one short post before you touch design settings; a live post is more useful than a perfect layout.

  • Go to Posts → Add New in the WordPress dashboard
  • Set a title and write your first few paragraphs
  • Click Publish — your blog is now live
