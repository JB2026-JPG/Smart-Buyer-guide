Cheap web hosting: what to check before buying.

Last checked: September 2026

An ultra-low headline price can end up costing more over 3 years due to steep renewal increases and expensive add-ons.

Hidden costs in cheap hosting

  • Renewal Hikes: A $2.99/mo promo rate often turns into $10.99/mo at renewal.
  • Paid Security & SSL: Make sure basic SSL security certificates aren't billed as paid upgrades.
  • Restoration Fees: Some cheap hosts charge extra fees if you need to restore a website backup.
