CRM REAL COST ANALYSIS

Pipedrive's starting price is only part of the real cost.

Pipedrive uses straightforward per-seat pricing, but the total cost can increase as your team grows or when you add tools such as LeadBooster, Projects, Campaigns, Web Visitors or Smart Docs.

Pricing checked today — September 13, 2026.
The prices shown on this page were checked against Pipedrive's publicly available pricing today. SaaS pricing can change at any time, and prices, plans, limits, discounts or fees may be different as soon as the next day. Always verify the provider's current pricing before you commit.

Pipedrive pricing today

LITE €14 Per seat/month when billed annually.
GROWTH €39 Per seat/month when billed annually.
PREMIUM €59 Per seat/month when billed annually.
ULTIMATE €79 Per seat/month when billed annually.

Pipedrive states that these prices are per seat per month when billed annually. Prices are VAT exclusive. EU customers may have VAT added according to the applicable rate.

Where Pipedrive's real cost can increase

  • More seats: because pricing is per seat, a growing sales team increases recurring cost.
  • Higher plans: advanced functionality requires moving to Growth, Premium or Ultimate.
  • Add-ons: additional products such as LeadBooster, Projects, Campaigns, Web Visitors and Smart Docs can increase the total cost.
  • VAT: Pipedrive states that prices are VAT exclusive and that EU customers can have VAT added.

Today's Pipedrive price can change

The prices on this page are a snapshot checked on September 13, 2026.

They should not be treated as a permanent price guarantee. Providers can change pricing, discounts, plan limits and add-ons.

In other words: a price that is correct today may already be different tomorrow.

Who should look closely at Pipedrive?

Pipedrive is particularly interesting for businesses that want a sales-focused CRM with clear per-seat pricing and a relatively simple pipeline-oriented approach.

The important question is not simply whether €14 per seat looks cheap. The better question is what your team will actually need as it grows.
