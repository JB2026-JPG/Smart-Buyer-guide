Current monday CRM pricing

USD pricing checked September 15, 2026.

monday CRM currently starts from $12 per seat/month on the Basic plan, billed annually ($18 per seat/month billed monthly). The exact price depends on the selected plan and team size.

Plans start from 3 seats, so the advertised per-seat starting price should not be treated as the total minimum bill for a very small team. Standard is priced at $17/seat/month and Pro at $28/seat/month, both billed annually.

monday CRM offers both monthly and annual billing. monday states that annual billing receives a discount compared with monthly billing.

For larger teams, monday's Ultimate tier is quoted directly by its sales team.

Prices do not include tax. The final price is determined by the customer's billing country and is shown before payment.

The SaaSRealCost verdict

monday CRM is interesting when flexibility is important. The main Real Cost question is whether the flexibility saves enough operational effort to justify the plan and seat structure you actually need.

Best suited for: teams that want CRM combined with flexible workflows and broader work-management functionality.

Watch carefully: minimum seat requirements, plan limits and the cost of moving into advanced tiers.
