Current monday CRM pricing

Prices checked September 13, 2026.

monday CRM pricing is based on the plan and number of users. Plans start from 3 users.

monday currently offers monthly and annual billing. Annual plans receive an 18% discount according to monday.

The official pricing page also states that customers purchasing more than 40 users should request a quote.

The SaaSRealCost verdict

monday CRM is interesting when flexibility is important. The main Real Cost question is whether the flexibility saves enough operational effort to justify the plan and user structure you actually need.

Best suited for: teams that want CRM combined with flexible workflows and broader work-management functionality.

Watch carefully: minimum user requirements, plan limits and the cost of moving into advanced tiers.
