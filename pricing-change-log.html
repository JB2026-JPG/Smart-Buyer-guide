SaaS Pricing Change Log

A transparent structure for recording pricing, plan and limit changes over time and translating them into real-cost impact.

Do not publish a pricing change without evidence. A change record should contain the old value, new value, effective/observed date, source, assumptions and real-cost impact.

Why this matters

A historical record creates an asset that ordinary review sites do not have. Over time, SaaSRealCost can show not only today's price but how pricing changed and what those changes meant for actual buyers.

SaaSRealCost provides independent research and calculators. Pricing changes. Verify current provider pricing before committing. Affiliate relationships may exist; editorial scoring is based on the published methodology.
