Pricing source & methodology

SaaSRealCost retrieves pricing from provider sources at the time the report is generated. Pricing can vary by country, currency, taxes, promotions, billing frequency, seat minimums and product configuration. The report is therefore an estimate based on the verified pricing returned at calculation time.
