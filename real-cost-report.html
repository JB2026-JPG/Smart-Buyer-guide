How pricing data works

SaaSRealCost automatically checks provider pricing pages and stores a price only when sufficient evidence is found for a recurring USD plan price.

If a provider temporarily blocks a request, returns a rate limit such as HTTP 429, or the pricing page cannot be reliably interpreted, the system does not replace an existing verified price with uncertain data.

Prices and plans can change. Always confirm the final price, billing terms, seat requirements and included features on the provider's official pricing page before purchasing.

Pricing data is collected automatically for comparison purposes. SaaSRealCost may receive affiliate commissions when users purchase through qualifying links.
