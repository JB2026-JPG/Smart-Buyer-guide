HubSpot Sales Hub pricing today

Current public pricing checked September 13, 2026

Free: $0/month for up to 2 users.

Starter: from $7/seat/month when billed annually. The displayed monthly commitment price is $20/seat/month.

Professional: from $90/seat/month when billed annually. A required one-time Professional onboarding fee of $1,500 applies.

Enterprise: from $150/seat/month. A required one-time Enterprise onboarding fee of $3,500 applies.

HubSpot also lists HubSpot Credits at $9 per 1,000 credits when paid annually.

Why HubSpot scores 7.4/10

Price — 8/10

8/10

The free plan is a strong entry point and Starter is relatively accessible. That keeps HubSpot from receiving a low price score.

Growth cost — 6/10

6/10

The jump from Starter to Professional and Enterprise is substantial. Growing teams can therefore experience a significant increase in recurring cost.

Hidden extras — 6/10

6/10

Required onboarding fees on Professional and Enterprise and usage-based HubSpot Credits mean the headline seat price does not tell the complete story.

Transparency — 8/10

8/10

HubSpot publishes extensive pricing and feature information. The downside is that multiple products, tiers, seats and credits can make the final cost harder to understand.

Ease of exit — 9/10

9/10

There is no unusual technical barrier that automatically prevents a normal CRM migration. The practical migration workload can still be significant.

The SaaSRealCost verdict

HubSpot is not necessarily expensive at the entry level. The issue is what happens when a company needs more advanced sales automation, reporting, AI functionality or higher-tier features.

That is why HubSpot scores well on entry price and transparency, but lower on growth cost and extras.

Best suited for: businesses that value a broad sales ecosystem and expect to use more than a basic CRM.

Watch carefully: onboarding requirements, higher tiers, additional seats and usage-based features.
