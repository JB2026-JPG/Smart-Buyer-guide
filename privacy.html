PRIVACY

Simple, minimal data use.

The Demand Finder uses public question data only to produce research signals.

What the site does

The Demand Finder requests public questions from the Stack Exchange API and processes their public titles in the visitor's browser. It does not need an account, API key, database or contact list.

Local browser storage

The dashboard can store its latest research results in your browser's local storage so you do not have to query the source on every refresh.

What it does not do

It does not ask for passwords or payment details, contact question authors, scrape profiles, or sell personal data.

Third-party services

When you click an external affiliate link, you leave this site and the destination provider's privacy and tracking policies apply.
