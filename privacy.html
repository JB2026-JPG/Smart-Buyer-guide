Simple, minimal data use.

SaaS Real Cost is designed to use only the information needed to provide and improve its buyer guides.

What the site does

SaaS Real Cost provides independent buyer guides and cost comparisons for software, hosting and related online services. The site may use basic website analytics to understand how visitors use the site and which pages are useful.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to measure website traffic and understand general usage patterns. Analytics data may include information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type and referral source.

Local browser storage

Some site features may store information locally in your browser so that the feature can work more efficiently. This information remains in your browser and is not used to identify you personally.

What it does not do

SaaS Real Cost does not ask visitors for passwords or payment details, does not contact people based on their activity on the site, and does not sell personal data.

Third-party services

When you click an external affiliate link, you leave this site and the destination provider's privacy, cookie and tracking policies apply. These providers may use tracking technologies to attribute purchases or referrals.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you purchase through an affiliate link, SaaS Real Cost may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate tracking is handled by the relevant third-party provider.
